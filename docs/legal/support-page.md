## Frequently asked questions

### What is DBF Fitness?
A private app for DBF members and coaches. Your coach builds your workout and nutrition plan, you
tick items off as you go, and you join your coach's live classes from your phone.

### Who can use it?
DBF members and DBF staff. You need an account, and a coach.

### How do I sign up?
Open the app, tap **Create account**, and enter your name, email and a password. Then **tick the box
agreeing to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy** — the **Create account** button stays greyed
out until you do. You can read both documents first by tapping their names in that sentence; they
open in your browser. Confirm your email, then sign in. If your gym gave you an account already,
just sign in.

### The "Create account" button is greyed out
The three most likely reasons, in order: the **agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy** box is not
ticked, a field is still empty, or the password is too short. The app shows a short message under
whichever one is the problem.

### The app is asking me to accept the Terms again
We do that whenever the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy change in a way that matters, so
nobody is held to a document they have not seen. Tap the links to read what changed, then
**Accept and continue**. If you would rather not accept, **Sign out** on the same screen; your
account and your training history stay exactly as they are, and you can accept next time you
sign in. Your membership is not affected either way.

### I didn't get the confirmation email
Check your spam folder, then use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen, or email
`{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` and we will resend it.

### How do I pick a coach?
Profile tab → **Choose your coach**. You can see each coach's bio and specialties and pick one who
is accepting members.

### How do I join a live class?
Community tab → **Live** → open the class → **Join**. Your phone will ask for camera and microphone
permission the first time. Classes only open near their start time.

### Why does it say I need an active membership?
Live classes are for members with an active DBF membership. If your payment is late you keep access
for a **10-day grace period** and you will see a reminder when you join. After that, live classes are
locked until your membership is up to date. Everything else in the app keeps working. Memberships are
arranged with DBF directly — talk to your coach or email `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`.

### Do I pay inside the app?
No. DBF memberships are paid directly to DBF, outside the app. The app never asks for card details.

### The camera or microphone doesn't work in a class
- iPhone: Settings → DBF Fitness → turn on **Camera** and **Microphone**.
- Android: Settings → Apps → DBF Fitness → Permissions → allow **Camera** and **Microphone**.
Then rejoin the class. You can also join with camera and microphone off and just watch.

### I'm not getting notifications
Allow notifications for DBF Fitness in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → DBF Fitness →
Notifications; Android: Settings → Apps → DBF Fitness → Notifications), then open the app once so it
can register your device.

### Are live classes recorded?
Not automatically. A coach may upload a recording of a class so that class notes can be made from
it, and must tell the class before recording. If you do not want to be recorded, tell your coach
before the class starts.

### How are class notes made?
From a coach's uploaded recording: an automated speech-to-text service produces a transcript, an AI
service drafts notes from that transcript, and **a coach reviews and edits the notes before they are
published**. Notes are general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

## Report abuse or objectionable content

We have **zero tolerance** for harassment, hate, sexual content, threats or any abusive behaviour —
in a live class, in your display name, or anywhere else in the app.

**In the app:**
1. Open the **Community** tab and open your group.
2. Find the person in the roster and tap **Report**.
3. Choose a reason — *Harassment*, *Spam*, *Inappropriate content* or *Other* — add details if you
   want (up to 500 characters) and send. You will see "Report sent. Thanks for keeping the group
   safe."

**Block someone:** same roster row → **Block** → **Yes, block**. You stop seeing each other in group
rosters immediately. Blocking is instant and does not need our approval.

**By email:** `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` — tell us who, what happened and when.

**What we do:** DBF staff review every report and act within **24 hours**. Depending on what
happened we may remove content, warn the person, remove them from a group or class, suspend them, or
delete their account. We act faster, without notice, when someone is at risk.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app:

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to **Danger zone** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your current password and type `DELETE` (capital letters) in the confirmation field.
4. Tap **Permanently delete**.

You are signed out immediately and the account is gone. **This cannot be undone.**

**What is deleted:** your login, your profile, your workout and exercise completions, your effort
and milestones, your diet check-ins, your group memberships, the blocks and reports you created,
your live-class attendance records, your push notification tokens, your membership record, and any
files you uploaded.

**What is not deleted:** content other people created that mentions you — for example a report
someone else filed about you, or a coach's class recording, transcript or notes in which you appear
— and copies in our backups until they age out. If you need to be removed from a coach's recording,
email `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`.

Deleting your app account does **not** cancel a DBF membership arranged outside the app. Contact DBF
to cancel that.

Prefer us to do it? Email `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` from your account's email address.
